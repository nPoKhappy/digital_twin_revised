Test comparison of models selected using validation data. (a) Interpolation and (b) near extrapolation. Each symbol is one model training seed (42, 43 or 44; 15 models per panel). KCI measures directional agreement with the shared artificial neural network (ANN) reference. The average relative root mean squared error (AvgRelRMSE) uses the corresponding No-Gain seed-42 test root mean squared error as denominator. KCI is evaluated on steady-state probe points; AvgRelRMSE uses dynamic test cases. Colours identify the model family; marker shapes identify the training seed. The dashed horizontal line is the No-Gain seed-42 baseline. Points are individual runs; no error bars or significance tests are shown.
