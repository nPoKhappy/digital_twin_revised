KCI grouped by ANN gain magnitude. Panels (a-d) show in-range test operating points; panels (e-h) show soft-OOD test operating points. The columns show Air2 to H2S, T2 to H2S, Air2 to SO2 and T2 to SO2, respectively. The key identifies the model family and its validation-selected parameters. KCI is the percentage of dynamic-model gain directions agreeing with the shared ANN reference, over operating points with absolute ANN gain at least 1e-5, two perturbation directions and 18 prediction steps; a zero dynamic gain counts as a disagreement. Points show the mean of model-training seeds 42, 43 and 44; error bars show plus or minus one sample standard deviation across these three seeds (n = 3). All three runs share the same ANN and test operating points. The bars describe training-seed variability, not a confidence interval. Markers are displaced horizontally within groups solely to improve visibility. SS denotes steady state and rho is the Difficult-GradNorm target ratio. Q1-Q4 are quartiles of normalized absolute ANN gain, calculated separately for each region and input-output pair using the existing test grouping. The source data and quartile-edge table provide the grouping thresholds and counts.
